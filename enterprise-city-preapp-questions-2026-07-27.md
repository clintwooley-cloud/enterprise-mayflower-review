# Enterprise / Mayflower pre-application question sheet

## Goal
Get an early yes/no on the **right entitlement path** before paying for full survey/civil/concept rework.

## 20-second setup
"We’re looking at a roughly 5.03-acre tract off Mayflower Street in Enterprise with about 101 feet of frontage and a diagonal creek/sewer corridor across the site. We’re evaluating a small coordinated residential project with permanent cottage-style units on foundations, likely using shared access/open space rather than a conventional lot-by-lot subdivision. Before we spend more money on design, we want staff direction on the right approval path and the likely practical unit range."

## Core questions for staff

### Parcel / zoning
1. What is the parcel’s **current zoning classification**?
2. Is the tract inside city limits and fully under **City of Enterprise** zoning/subdivision jurisdiction?
3. Are there any overlay districts, corridor rules, or other special restrictions affecting this tract?

### Best entitlement path
4. For a coordinated cottage/cabin-style residential project on this tract, would staff steer us toward:
   - cottage development,
   - multifamily,
   - PRD,
   - condominium/site-condo,
   - another path?
5. If staff sees this as **cottage development**, does the ordinance language limiting the project to **not more than ten dwelling lots without street frontage** effectively cap the project below the 18–20 unit range?
6. If staff sees this as **multifamily/condo**, what review path would apply first: development plan, rezoning, special exception, preliminary plat, or some combination?
7. Would the City consider a **sub-10-acre PRD** here given the frontage constraint and drainage/easement corridor?

### Access / circulation / fire
8. With about **101 feet of frontage**, is a **single controlled entrance** the expected/acceptable access pattern?
9. Would an internal **private street/common drive** layout be acceptable in principle?
10. What are the main **fire department expectations** we need to design around for a one-entry project like this?
11. Is a second access likely to be required at any unit count we should be thinking about?

### Parking / site design
12. For a small one-bedroom/two-bedroom cottage-style development, what parking expectations should we assume beyond the base ordinance ratios?
13. Would staff expect clustered parking, garages, guest parking, or any special screening/layout treatment?
14. At what point does the site start to look too intense from the City’s perspective even if the math technically fits?

### Utilities / drainage / constraints
15. Are there known **floodplain, drainage, creek-buffer, or utility-easement** issues on this tract beyond what is visible from the basic concept?
16. Would stormwater detention likely need to be fully accommodated on-site?
17. Are there utility-capacity concerns for sewer or water service in this immediate area?

### Process / submittals
18. Before a formal submittal, would staff prefer a **sketch review / informal meeting**?
19. What materials would be most useful for that first conversation:
   - aerial + parcel sketch,
   - rough concept,
   - survey,
   - utility info,
   - narrative?
20. If we pursue this, what is the most efficient sequence:
   - staff meeting,
   - rezoning/PRD or development-plan application,
   - preliminary plat,
   - fire/civil review?

## What we want to leave the conversation with
- the **correct entitlement bucket**
- whether **18 units** is even realistic
- whether **20 units** is plausible
- whether the City sees **22 units as too aggressive**
- whether we need to think **PRD / multifamily condo** instead of cottage subdivision
- the **first paid consultant step** that actually matters

## My suggested posture on the call
- Do **not** lead with 22 units.
- Lead with **"small coordinated cottage-style residential project"**.
- Ask the City what bucket **they** think it belongs in.
- Then test **18 units first**, **20 second**, and only mention **22** as a conceptual upper-end scenario.
